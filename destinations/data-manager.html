Data Services

Data Manager

As a Data Manager, you lead the management of the academy's management information system (MIS), ensuring data integrity across all school systems. You oversee statutory data returns, manage assessment data cycles, produce reports for senior leaders and governors, and lead the data team to support school improvement through accurate, timely information.

£28,000 - £35,000 3-5 year journey Primary & Secondary

Role overview

Salary Range £28,000 - £35,000
Typical Journey 3-5 years from Data Officer
Sector Primary, Secondary, MAT
Qualification MIS certification / data skills
Reports to School Business Manager / SLT

Your journey to Data Manager

The typical career stops on the way to this destination

Stop 1

School Administration Role

Begin in school admin where you gain experience with the school MIS, student records, and data entry. Develop strong spreadsheet skills and an understanding of the school data calendar including census returns.

Duration: 1-2 years Focus: MIS & data entry skills
Stop 2

Data Administrator / Officer

Move into a dedicated data role. Take responsibility for maintaining student records, processing assessment data, supporting census returns, and producing reports. Build expertise in the school MIS and develop your understanding of statutory data requirements.

Duration: 2-3 years Focus: MIS expertise & statutory returns
Stop 3

Senior Data Officer

Take on additional responsibilities such as leading on census returns, managing timetable data, training staff on MIS use, and producing analytical reports for SLT. Begin developing expertise in data analysis tools and assessment systems.

Duration: 1-2 years Focus: Analysis & strategic reporting
Stop 4 - Final Destination

Data Manager

You've arrived at your destination. As Data Manager, you lead the school's data function, manage the MIS, oversee all statutory returns, produce strategic reports for governors and Ofsted, line-manage the data team, and ensure data drives school improvement at every level.

Salary: £28,000 - £35,000

Skills and qualities you'll need

The personal attributes that will help you thrive in this role

💻

MIS Expertise

Advanced knowledge of school MIS platforms (SIMS, Arbor, Bromcom) including configuration, reporting, and troubleshooting.

📊

Data Analysis

Able to analyse assessment data, attendance trends, and pupil demographics to produce meaningful insights for school leaders.

🔎

Attention to Detail

Meticulous accuracy when managing student data, census returns, and statutory reporting where errors can have regulatory consequences.

👥

Team Leadership

Experience of managing data staff, training colleagues on MIS use, and coordinating data tasks across the school.

📋

Statutory Knowledge

Understanding of DfE data requirements, school census, CTF transfers, UPNs, GDPR, and the annual data calendar.

💬

Communication

Able to translate complex data into clear, actionable reports and presentations for non-technical audiences including governors.
