Receptionist

As a school Receptionist, you are the welcoming face and first point of contact for everyone who visits or calls the academy. You manage the front desk, handle visitor sign-in procedures, answer telephone enquiries, and support the admin team with day-to-day tasks. Your role is vital to safeguarding and creating a positive first impression of the school.

£18,000 - £22,000 Entry level All phases

Role overview

Salary Range £18,000 - £22,000
Typical Journey Direct entry / 6 months experience
Sector Primary, Secondary, Special
Qualification GCSEs; Level 2 Admin desirable
Reports to Office Manager

Your journey to Receptionist

The typical career stops on the way to this destination

Stop 1

GCSEs & Basic Skills

Achieve good GCSEs in English and Maths. Develop your IT skills, particularly with Microsoft Office. Any customer service experience from part-time work or volunteering is valuable preparation for this people-facing role.

Duration: Ongoing Focus: English, Maths, IT
Stop 2

Customer Service or Admin Experience

Gain experience in a customer-facing or administrative role. This could be in retail, hospitality, healthcare reception, or general office work. The key is developing your telephone manner, people skills, and ability to multitask under pressure.

Duration: 6-12 months Focus: Customer service skills
Stop 3 - Final Destination

School Receptionist

You've arrived at your destination. As the school Receptionist, you manage the front desk, greet visitors, check identification, maintain the single central record of visitors, handle phone calls, distribute messages, and support the wider admin team. You play a key frontline role in the school's safeguarding procedures.

Salary: £18,000 - £22,000

Skills and qualities you'll need

The personal attributes that will help you thrive in this role

👋

Welcoming Manner

A warm, friendly, and professional approach that makes every visitor, parent, and pupil feel welcome and valued from the moment they arrive.

📞

Telephone Skills

Confident and clear on the phone, able to handle high call volumes, take accurate messages, and direct enquiries to the right person efficiently.

💪

Calm Under Pressure

School reception can be hectic. The ability to stay composed, prioritise tasks, and manage competing demands with a smile is essential.

🔒

Safeguarding Awareness

Understanding the importance of visitor management, ID checks, and following safeguarding protocols. You are a vital part of keeping children safe.

💻

IT Skills

Competent with visitor management systems, school MIS, email, and office software. Quick to learn new digital tools and processes.

🤝

Discretion

You will overhear and handle sensitive information daily. Being trustworthy and maintaining confidentiality is non-negotiable in this role.
