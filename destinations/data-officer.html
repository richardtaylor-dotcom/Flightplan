Data Services

Data Officer

As a Data Officer, you manage the day-to-day operation of the school's management information system, maintain student records, process assessment data, and support statutory data returns. You ensure data accuracy across the school and provide reports to support teaching staff, pastoral teams, and senior leaders in their decision-making.

£22,000 - £27,000 1-2 year journey Primary & Secondary

Role overview

Salary Range £22,000 - £27,000
Typical Journey 1-2 years from admin role
Sector Primary, Secondary, MAT
Qualification GCSEs / MIS experience
Reports to Data Manager / SBM

Your journey to Data Officer

The typical career stops on the way to this destination

Stop 1

School Admin or Data Entry Role

Begin in school administration or a data entry role where you develop proficiency with the school MIS, spreadsheets, and database management. Strong IT skills and attention to detail are essential foundations.

Duration: 1-2 years Focus: MIS & data skills
Stop 2 - Final Destination

Data Officer

You've arrived at your destination. As Data Officer, you maintain student records, process assessment data, support census returns, generate reports for staff and leaders, and ensure the MIS is accurate and up to date throughout the academic year.

Salary: £22,000 - £27,000

Skills and qualities you'll need

The personal attributes that will help you thrive in this role

💻

MIS Proficiency

Confident navigating and maintaining school MIS platforms, managing student records, and running reports.

🔎

Accuracy

Meticulous attention to detail when entering and verifying student data, assessment marks, and personal information.

📊

Spreadsheet Skills

Proficient with Excel or Google Sheets for data manipulation, pivot tables, and creating reports from raw data.

🔒

Data Protection

Understanding of GDPR requirements and the importance of handling sensitive student and family data responsibly.

📅

Time Management

Able to manage competing deadlines including census returns, assessment windows, and ad-hoc data requests from staff.

💬

Helpfulness

Approachable and patient when supporting teaching staff with MIS queries, report requests, and data questions.
