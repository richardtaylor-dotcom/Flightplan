Attendance Administrator

As an Attendance Administrator, you provide essential administrative support to the attendance team. You process attendance data, update registers, prepare letters to families, and produce attendance reports. This entry-level role is ideal for someone looking to build a career in school attendance and student welfare.

£19,000 - £23,000 Entry-level role Primary & Secondary

Role overview

Salary Range £19,000 - £23,000
Typical Journey Entry-level / 0-1 years
Sector Primary, Secondary, MAT
Qualification GCSEs / basic IT skills
Reports to Attendance Officer / Manager

Your journey to Attendance Administrator

The typical career stops on the way to this destination

Stop 1

Basic Admin Skills

Develop core administration skills including data entry, letter writing, and familiarity with office software. GCSEs in English and Maths are typically required. Any previous office or customer service experience is beneficial.

Duration: 0-1 year Focus: IT & admin fundamentals
Stop 2 - Final Destination

Attendance Administrator

You've arrived at your destination. As Attendance Administrator, you process daily registers, prepare attendance letters, update the MIS with absence codes, and produce reports for the attendance team. This is an excellent entry point into school-based attendance work.

Salary: £19,000 - £23,000

Skills and qualities you'll need

The personal attributes that will help you thrive in this role

🔎

Accuracy

Precise data entry skills for updating attendance registers and codes, where errors can affect statutory reporting.

💻

IT Skills

Comfortable using school MIS systems, spreadsheets, and word processing to manage attendance data and produce letters.

📅

Organisation

Able to manage daily administrative tasks efficiently, keeping records up to date and ensuring nothing falls through the cracks.

🔒

Confidentiality

Trusted to handle sensitive information about students and families with appropriate discretion and professionalism.

💬

Communication

Clear written communication for drafting attendance letters and professional verbal communication when dealing with families.

💪

Reliability

Dependable and consistent in completing daily tasks, as attendance monitoring requires action every single school day.
