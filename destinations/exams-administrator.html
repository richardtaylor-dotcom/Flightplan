Exams Administrator

As an Exams Administrator, you provide essential administrative support to the exams team during busy examination periods. You assist with data entry for exam entries, prepare exam materials, coordinate invigilation schedules, and help maintain the secure storage and distribution of exam papers in line with JCQ requirements.

£19,000 - £23,000 Entry-level role Secondary & Post-16

Role overview

Salary Range £19,000 - £23,000
Typical Journey Entry-level / 0-1 years
Sector Secondary, Sixth Form, FE
Qualification GCSEs / admin experience
Reports to Exams Officer / Exams Manager

Your journey to Exams Administrator

The typical career stops on the way to this destination

Stop 1

Basic Admin Skills & Qualifications

Develop your core admin skills including data entry, filing, and IT proficiency. Good GCSEs including English and Maths are typically required. Any prior office or administration experience is beneficial.

Duration: 0-1 year Focus: Core admin & IT skills
Stop 2 - Final Destination

Exams Administrator

You've arrived at your destination. As an Exams Administrator, you support the smooth running of exam seasons by handling data entry, preparing materials, assisting with invigilation coordination, and ensuring exam papers are securely managed. This is an excellent entry point into the examinations career pathway.

Salary: £19,000 - £23,000

Skills and qualities you'll need

The personal attributes that will help you thrive in this role

🔎

Accuracy

Careful and precise when entering data, checking candidate details, and handling exam materials where mistakes can have serious consequences.

📅

Organisation

Able to manage multiple tasks, keep materials in order, and work to strict deadlines during busy exam periods.

🔒

Confidentiality

Trusted to handle sensitive exam materials and student data with the highest levels of discretion and security.

💻

IT Skills

Comfortable with spreadsheets, databases, and school MIS systems for data entry and report generation.

🤝

Teamwork

Works well as part of a small team, supporting colleagues during the intense pressure of examination seasons.

💪

Reliability

Dependable and punctual, especially during exam seasons when deadlines are non-negotiable and consistency is critical.
