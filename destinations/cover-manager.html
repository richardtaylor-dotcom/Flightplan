School Operations

Cover Manager

As a Cover Manager, you coordinate the daily cover arrangements for absent teaching staff across the academy. You manage internal cover supervisors, book supply teachers, ensure lessons are covered with appropriate work, and maintain records of staff absence and cover costs. This is a fast-paced, early-morning role that keeps the school running smoothly every day.

£22,000 - £28,000 1-3 year journey Secondary

Role overview

Salary Range £22,000 - £28,000
Typical Journey 1-3 years from admin/cover supervisor
Sector Secondary, Sixth Form
Qualification GCSEs / school experience
Reports to Deputy Headteacher / SBM

Your journey to Cover Manager

The typical career stops on the way to this destination

Stop 1

Cover Supervisor or School Admin

Start as a cover supervisor delivering pre-set lessons in absent teachers' classrooms, or work in the school office to understand timetables, staffing, and daily operations. Build relationships with teaching staff and understand the school's curriculum structure.

Duration: 1-2 years Focus: Classroom experience & timetabling
Stop 2 - Final Destination

Cover Manager

You've arrived at your destination. As Cover Manager, you arrive early each morning to arrange cover for absent staff, manage a team of cover supervisors, liaise with supply agencies, ensure work is set for covered lessons, and track absence and cover data for senior leaders.

Salary: £22,000 - £28,000

Skills and qualities you'll need

The personal attributes that will help you thrive in this role

⏱

Early Riser

Cover must be arranged before school starts, so you need to be calm and decisive in the early hours when absence notifications arrive.

📅

Timetable Knowledge

Strong understanding of the school timetable, staffing structure, and who is available to cover which lessons and when.

💪

Problem Solving

Quick thinking to find solutions when multiple staff are absent, supply is unavailable, and cover needs reorganising at short notice.

💬

Diplomacy

Tactful when asking staff to provide cover, managing competing priorities, and dealing with supply agency negotiations.

👥

Staff Management

Experience of coordinating and managing a team of cover supervisors, ensuring they are deployed effectively and supported.

📊

Data Tracking

Able to maintain accurate records of staff absence, cover arrangements, and costs to inform budget planning and SLT reports.
