Examinations

Invigilator

As an Invigilator, you supervise students during examination sessions to ensure exams are conducted fairly and in accordance with JCQ regulations. You help distribute and collect exam papers, monitor candidates throughout the session, and report any irregularities to the Lead Invigilator or Exams Officer. This is a flexible, term-time role ideal for those seeking part-time work in education.

£11 - £13/hr Entry-level role Secondary & Post-16

Role overview

Salary Range £11 - £13/hr
Typical Journey Entry-level / no prior experience
Sector Secondary, Sixth Form, FE

Your journey to Invigilator

The typical career stops on the way to this destination

Stop 1

Application & DBS Check

Apply to your local school or academy trust. You will need to complete an enhanced DBS check and provide references. No formal qualifications are typically required, making this an accessible entry point into school-based work.

Duration: 2-4 weeks Focus: Safeguarding clearance
Stop 2 - Final Destination

Invigilator

You've arrived at your destination. After completing your in-house JCQ training, you will supervise exam sessions, distribute papers, monitor candidates, and help maintain exam conditions. This flexible role is typically busiest during the November, January, and May/June exam windows.

Salary: £11 - £13/hr

Skills and qualities you'll need

The personal attributes that will help you thrive in this role

👀

Attentiveness

Able to remain alert and observant throughout exam sessions, monitoring candidates carefully without being intrusive.

💪

Reliability

Punctual and dependable - exam sessions cannot start without invigilators, so attendance and timekeeping are critical.

😶

Calm Demeanour

Maintains a quiet, reassuring presence in the exam room to help students feel calm and focused during their exams.

🔒

Integrity

Trusted to uphold exam conditions impartially and report any concerns or irregularities without hesitation.

⏱

Time Awareness

Conscious of exam timings, reading time, and extra time allowances to support the smooth running of each session.

🚶

Physical Stamina

Comfortable standing and walking quietly for extended periods during exam sessions which can last several hours.
