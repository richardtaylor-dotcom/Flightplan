Deputy Attendance Officer

As a Deputy Attendance Officer, you support the Attendance Officer in monitoring and improving student attendance across the academy. You assist with first-day absence calls, update attendance records, chase late arrivals, and help coordinate attendance interventions for students with emerging patterns of absence.

£20,000 - £24,000 0-1 year journey Primary & Secondary

Your journey to Deputy Attendance Officer

The typical career stops on the way to this destination

Stop 1

School Reception or Admin Role

Gain experience in a school office environment where you answer phones, greet visitors, and manage student records. This gives you familiarity with the MIS system and daily school operations that underpin attendance work.

Duration: 0-1 year Focus: School admin & MIS systems
Stop 2 - Final Destination

Deputy Attendance Officer

You've arrived at your destination. As Deputy Attendance Officer, you support daily attendance monitoring, make absence calls, update registers, liaise with form tutors, and assist with attendance meetings. This role is an excellent stepping stone to Attendance Officer.

Salary: £20,000 - £24,000

Skills and qualities you'll need

The personal attributes that will help you thrive in this role

📞

Telephone Manner

Professional and friendly on the phone when contacting families about absences, even when conversations become difficult.

📝

Accuracy

Careful and precise when updating attendance codes, recording reasons for absence, and maintaining student records.

💻

MIS Proficiency

Comfortable navigating the school MIS system to run registers, update codes, and generate basic attendance reports.

🧡

Empathy

Understands that families face varied and sometimes complex challenges, and approaches attendance conversations with sensitivity.

⏱

Time Management

Able to prioritise tasks during busy mornings when registers need checking, calls need making, and late students need signing in.

🤝

Teamwork

Works well alongside the Attendance Officer, pastoral team, and reception staff to ensure seamless attendance monitoring.
