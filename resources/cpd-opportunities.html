CPD & Training

Professional development opportunities through Leigh Academies Trust and KM Training. Whether you are at the start of your career or looking to reach the next level, there are funded programmes, accredited courses, and collaborative networks to support your growth.

Trust CPD Programme

Leigh Academies Trust runs a comprehensive CPD programme designed for all staff across the trust. With a wide variety of formats and topics, there is something for everyone regardless of your role or experience level.

  • Twilight sessions on pedagogy, wellbeing, and specialist topics
  • INSET days with trust-wide and academy-specific focus
  • Trust-wide conferences bringing together professionals from every academy
  • Subject network meetings for curriculum collaboration
  • Available to both teaching and support staff at all levels

KM Training

KM Training is the trust's dedicated training arm, offering a broad range of accredited courses, apprenticeships, and bespoke training packages. It also delivers the School Direct initial teacher training programme, providing an excellent route into teaching for career changers and graduates.

  • Accredited courses across education and business disciplines
  • Apprenticeships from Level 2 to degree level
  • Bespoke training tailored to individual academy needs
  • School Direct ITT for aspiring teachers and career changers
  • Programmes for teachers, support staff, and leaders

NPQ Programmes

National Professional Qualifications are delivered through the trust, providing structured leadership development that is nationally recognised. These programmes are fully funded and designed to be completed part-time alongside your current role.

  • NPQ in Leading Teaching (NPQLT)
  • NPQ in Senior Leadership (NPQSL)
  • NPQ in Headship (NPQH)
  • NPQ in Executive Leadership (NPQEL)
  • Fully funded with no cost to participants
  • Studied part-time alongside your existing role

Apprenticeships

The trust offers a range of apprenticeship programmes funded through the apprenticeship levy, meaning there is no cost to staff. These provide nationally recognised qualifications while you continue working in your role.

  • Teaching Assistant apprenticeships (Level 3)
  • Business Administration apprenticeships (Level 3)
  • Leadership and Management apprenticeships (Level 5)
  • Funded through the apprenticeship levy at no cost to staff
  • Earn while you learn with on-the-job training

Subject Networks

Regular subject-specific meetings bring together practitioners from across the trust's academies. These networks are a powerful way to share expertise, improve consistency, and develop your subject knowledge alongside colleagues who share your passion.

  • Regular meetings across all curriculum subjects
  • Best practice sharing between trust academies
  • Collaborative moderation of student work
  • Joint curriculum planning and resource development
  • Opportunities to lead and present to peers

Leadership Development

The trust provides tailored leadership development pathways for every stage of your career. Whether you are considering your first leadership step or looking to move into senior roles, there is structured support to help you get there.

  • Aspiring leaders programme for those ready for their first step
  • Middle leadership development with coaching and mentoring
  • Senior leadership coaching and strategic development
  • Tailored pathways matched to your career goals
  • Cross-trust exposure and secondment opportunities
